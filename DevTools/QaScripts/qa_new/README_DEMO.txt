EMPLOYEES TAKE REVENGE - friends' demo (floors 1 to 4)

How to play
 1. Start Steam first and stay logged in: the game uses Steam for lobbies, friends and invites.
 2. Unzip the WHOLE folder somewhere (do not run the .exe from inside the zip) and double-click EmployeesTakeRevenge.exe.
    If Windows shows "unknown publisher", click More info, then Run anyway.
 3. One player hosts: PLAY, then Public, Code only or Friends only; press START RUN when everybody is in.
    The others join: PLAY, then Browse public lobbies, or Join by code, or accept a Steam invite. Up to 5 players.
    Everybody must run exactly this build.
 4. The controls are in SETTINGS > KEYBINDS (and in a run: Esc > Keybinds).
 5. Every run starts at the Loading Docks with a short cinematic: you and a coworker watch the boss on the wall TV. Hold SPACE for a second to skip it
    (with friends, half of the party has to hold it).
 6. Hold Z to scan the room you stand in: the valuables in it light up for a few seconds. The loot is watches, phones, wallets and the like, plus a few
    big things that take two people to carry (Q grabs). Follow the "Security Office" signs to the guards' outpost: its computer lists where the safes are
    and their codes (the log-in is on a post-it on the monitor).
 7. The way back down to the Loading Docks opens only once the floor's quota is met.
 8. Voice chat is proximity voice: you only hear a teammate who is close (clear within about 4 m, fading out by about 18 m, M mutes your mic).
    Nobody can be heard across a floor yet; walkie-talkies, sold at the vendor, will come later.
 9. The demo ends after floor 4. The vendor at the Loading Docks gives away a pew pistol for free once floor 3 is done (one also lies in floor 3's
    guard outpost): try it on the training dummy. The guards are armed from floor 4.

If something goes wrong
 - No lobbies or friends show up: make sure Steam is running before you start the game.
 - The game keeps its log here: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\DefaultCompany\Employees Take Revenge\Player.log
   Send that file with a description of what happened.
