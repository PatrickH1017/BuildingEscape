Floor 7 environmental art pass - before / after pictures (2026-10-23)

WHAT THESE ARE (read this first)
These are NOT Unity screenshots. Nobody has run the dressed floor in Unity. They are drawn outside Unity,
by a small renderer, from the REAL planned layout of Floor 7 (seed 4101: every wall, doorway, piece and prop
position the planner produces) with the REAL Synty pack models, plain lighting and no game materials, no
ceiling and no lamps. Colours, shadows and the mood will differ in the game. What the pictures DO show
faithfully: where things are, how big they are, and how much room is left to walk.

Every picture is a BEFORE (left, orange) and AFTER (right, green) pair taken from exactly the same camera
position and direction (eye height 1.6 m).

01_main_rooms_*          Representative furnished rooms, from the same corner before and after:
                         an office (two views of the same one), lounge, canteen, long open-plan hall,
                         break room, second office, conference room, open-plan area, storage, copy room,
                         nook, second lounge, training room.
                         (The server room is shown in 02, from its doorway; the corner view I first tried
                         looked at a bare wall, so it is left out.)
02_doorways_*            The view of the doorway of nine rooms from about 5 m away (office, lounge, storage, copy
                         room, break room, canteen, conference room, server room, training room): the
                         places where placement had to be careful. Nothing is placed in a doorway (the rules keep
                         solid things 0.9 m and small things 0.3 m away from it).
03_halls_and_big_things_* Two corridor views (the lightly decorated corridors) and the five solid things the
                         pass added (arcade machine, floor plant, fish tank, two carts), each seen from where
                         a player would walk past.
04_plan_whole_floor      Top-down plan of the whole floor, before above and after below. Green = where a
                         1.2 m wide walker can stand; orange = new furniture; amber = new things on the floor;
                         green dots = on furniture; pink = on walls. Red would mean floor the new things cut
                         off: 0 cells. Yellow = floor the new furniture itself takes: 777 cells of 0.1 m.
04_plan_offices_zoom, 04_plan_lounge_arcade_zoom   The same plan, zoomed on two areas.
