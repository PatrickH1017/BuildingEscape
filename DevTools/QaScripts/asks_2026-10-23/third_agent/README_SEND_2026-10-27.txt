EMPLOYEES TAKE REVENGE - friends' playtest build

How to play
 1. Start Steam first and stay logged in: the game uses Steam for lobbies, friends and invites.
 2. Unzip the WHOLE folder somewhere (do not run the .exe from inside the zip) and double-click EmployeesTakeRevenge.exe.
    If Windows shows "unknown publisher", click More info, then Run anyway.
 3. One player hosts: PLAY, then Public, Code only or Friends only; press START RUN when everybody is in.
    The others join: PLAY, then Browse public lobbies, or Join by code, or accept a Steam invite. Up to 5 players.
    Everybody must run exactly this build.
 4. The controls are in SETTINGS > KEYBINDS (and in a run: Esc > Keybinds). Master volume and Music volume are separate sliders in SETTINGS (the Esc menu has a Settings button too).
 5. Every run starts at the Loading Docks with a short cinematic: you and your coworkers watch the boss on the TV in the break room, and then the vendor shows up. Hold SPACE for a second to skip it
    (with friends, half of the party has to hold it).
 6. The "PLAYER 1, PLAYER 2 ..." badge at the top right is your number. The same number is on the Nav map of the security computer, so the player at
    the computer can say "Player 2, go left".
 7. Press Z to scan the room you stand in (hold it and the scan repeats; there are 2 seconds between two scans; pressing it before the next one is ready only makes an error sound, and the only pop-up of a scan is "Item Marked", when it marked something): what is in it glows for a few seconds - safes and valuables gold, weapons red, cash green. Follow the "Security Office" signs to the guards' outpost
    (hack the keypad beside its door with E, then W A S D as shown). Inside is the security computer: the log-in is on a post-it on its monitor.
    Its tabs, in this order: NAV (a plan of the floor: a round numbered badge for
    every player, a red diamond for every officer, a ring that spreads from anybody who is moving, a padlock and an orange edge on a locked room, the elevators E and G,
    the stairwells S, the green room named SECURITY (the guards' outpost: it has no room number); the mouse wheel zooms, dragging moves the plan, pointing at a room names it
    and a click opens its card with a LOCK / UNLOCK button; a little terminal sits under the plan), SAFES (the safes of the floor you are on, and no other floor's: each one's room and its code) and COMMANDS (the same terminal, bigger). In the terminal type a room
    number (or the word Security for the outpost's own door) and press Enter: the doors of that room
    shut and nobody, officers included, can open them; type the same number again and they open again (the orange marks on the plan are the locked rooms).
 8. Shutting the outpost's door locks nothing: the officers open it like any other door. To lock yourself in, type SECURITY in the computer's terminal:
    now the officers cannot open it either, and somebody outside has to hack its keypad again to open it (typing SECURITY again, or E on the door from
    inside, undoes it yourself).
 9. Weapons: the Tab window lists the weapons in your pack with EQUIP / PUT AWAY and DROP for each; the number key of a weapon (1 to 6, as on the bar at the bottom) takes it into your hand and, pressed again while it is in your hand, puts it away; G drops the weapon in your hand.
    Melee weapons are on the desk in the guards' outpost (two on floor 1, a chance of one on the others); one crowbar also lies somewhere on floor 1 outside the outpost. Empty hands: the left mouse
    button punches. The player's pew has a silencer on the end of its barrel: the shot is only heard 12 m away; the officers' pews have none and are loud.
    The pew pistol (8 rounds in the gun; the bullets you carry reload it, there is no limit on them; four bullets drop an officer with no shield, six one with a light shield) is locked in a gun safe on the desk in floor 3's outpost (E on it: hold the left mouse button on the green circle and drag along the corridor to the red one; touching a wall sets off the alarm); once floor 3 is done the
    dealer in the Loading Docks sells the pistol and bullets for money. In the Loading Docks the ammunition is unlimited; on the floors the
    elevator does NOT refill your guns, so buy bullets.
    Sprinting uses stamina (the bar at the bottom of the screen); when it runs dry you are winded for a moment.
10. The officers notice you faster the closer you are and take longer the further away you are.
11. The way back down to the Loading Docks opens only once the floor's quota is met. The demo ends after floor 6.
12. Every floor has a safe. Floors 1 and 2 have one safe, full of cash (bank the bundles in the elevator). Floor 3 has two safes and floors 4, 5 and 6 three - every one in a different numbered room.
    A safe on floors 3 to 6 holds a gun only some of the time (about 4 times in 10 when it holds no armour; on floors 5 and 6 it is not always the pew) and may hold nothing at all; it sometimes holds a little cash and a spare clip of bullets as well:
    each is its own item, so aim at it and press E for each. Any safe, the cash ones on floors 1 and 2 too, may also hold a gold watch (about one in three). A clip's bullets go into your gun's reserve (no gun, no clip). The player at the
    computer reads the rooms and the codes on the SAFES tab (it does not say what is inside; every safe's code is there)
    and sends the team; the keypad takes the 4-digit code (E on the safe), or a crowbar pries a safe open when the code is not on record (it snaps now and then).
    The SAFES tab and the journal (J) only ever show the safes of the floor you are on.
    An officer's vision cone is only drawn while you can see the officer. The backup comes faster the higher you climb. A body goes away after a while.
13. The Loading Docks has a break room (the boss's message plays on its TV) and a PVP room: inside the PVP room a health bar floats over every
    head, fists, weapons and bullets hurt the other players, and whoever loses is knocked out for a few seconds and then gets up again.
14. If you are knocked out for the floor while teammates are still going you can watch them: A / D or the mouse buttons switch between them.
15. The security computer's Nav map shows the hallway loudspeakers as grey M tiles: click one to switch it off or on (Select all / Deselect all too).
    Hold V while signed in at the computer and your voice comes out of the speakers that are on, all over the floor - and every officer within earshot
    of one that is on rushes to the outpost. Switch off the speakers near the officers first. The microphone on the desk next to the computer lights
    up green while you are live, and your character reaches out to its button (stand close to the desk to see the hand on it).
16. The menus and the HUD are blue now (they were purple), with thinner outlines, softer shadows and a quieter shine.
17. Officers and guns. A bullet damages an officer and never knocks him over: four pew bullets drop an officer with no shield, six one with a light shield (a vest on his chest); an officer who is shot while he holds somebody
    down lets go of them. A melee blow knocks an officer over for a few seconds (the bat 3, the crowbar 4, the hatchet 6) and never knocks him out. From floor 3 some officers carry pews and wear a light shield, the others nothing.
    An officer who goes down sometimes drops a few bullets (none most of the time, up to three): run over them to take them (you need a gun they fit). An officer's bullet knocks you down for a moment.
18. Armour. A safe on floors 3 to 6 may hold a piece of Light Armour instead of a gun. It goes into your pack like any item (5.5 lbs) and you wear it while it is in the pack - the better of two pieces (a second one is a very heavy spare to bring to a teammate), and a vest shows on your character.
    An officer's bullet then knocks you down for 75 % of the usual time. Carry the piece back to the Loading Docks and the dealer sells Light Armour to the whole party ($600).
19. The journal (J) opens on its CHECKLIST tab (SAFE NOTES is the second tab; "Press J for journal" is at the bottom left of the screen, with "Press Tab for inventory" above it), and the checklist lists: All valuables collected, All unique items collected, All safes opened, All cash collected and Outpost hacked (the lock of the guards' outpost is hacked open). When you have collected all of one kind its box is ticked in green and the line is crossed out in green.
20. The gun wall behind the dealer in the Loading Docks fills up as you unlock guns: a gun hangs there once it is unlocked (the pew once he sells it).
21. Officers. An officer needs one second next to you to pin you. When an officer goes down for good another comes out of a stairwell 15 seconds later, and an officer who sees a body raises the alarm. Take hold of a body with Q (Q again lets go)
    and drag it away to hide it; you walk slower while you drag it. Hold Alt (or V) to look around: a tap does nothing.
22. The four throwable things (Coffee Mug, Heavy Stapler, Squeaky Toy, Fire Extinguisher) are sold by the vending machines of the Loading Docks (E on one), not by the dealer. The dealer sells a walkie talkie: hold it out (equip it) and talk to a teammate who holds one out too, at any distance.
23. The ammunition panel shows the rounds in your gun and all the bullets you carry; a ring fills up in it while you reload. Weapons weigh about half what they did, Drop all loot drops everything worth money, and the elevator's buttons rise from left to right.
24. The other players' names float over their heads (their Steam names).
25. At the security computer move the mouse off the monitor to look around slowly (it turns back when the mouse is over the monitor again); with the microphone in view and the mouse off the monitor, E speaks into it like V does.
26. The PVP room is dressed as a battlefield now.
27. Windows are kept clear: nothing hangs on the windows of the outside walls or on an office's glass front, and the corridor outside a glass office has no cabinets or shelves in front of it (a water cooler may stand there). No clocks on the cubicle panels.
28. The microphone pill under your player badge (top right) shows your mic, in a solo run too: M mutes it.
29. SETTINGS (and the Esc menu's Settings page) has AUDIO DEVICES: MICROPHONE is a list of the microphones Windows reports (DEFAULT is the one Windows and Steam use; a microphone you pick is used instead, and its voice is sent as plain
    audio, which takes more bandwidth than Steam's); SPEAKERS has a button that opens Windows' own "App volume and device preferences" page, where you pick the output device of this game (the game itself cannot switch it).
30. The Esc menu's cards now stand between the floor's banner at the top and the hotbar at the bottom (the Settings page scrolls). The Esc menu has a LEAVE RUN button in a solo run too (it takes you back to the menu), as it does in a team run.
31. Marks and the Big Alarm. A guard who spots a player marks THAT player and nobody else; a second player who is spotted is marked too. When half of the party is marked (both players of a duo; a solo player is the whole party, so his first sighting counts)
    the Big Alarm goes on: there is no turning it off (the alarm panel cannot), you have to make the quota and fight your way out. If the Big Alarm is not on and the marked players are all pinned, the alert goes off and no more officers are called.
    Guards are capped: 6 alive at once on floors 1 and 2, 8 with the Big Alarm; 13 from floor 3 on. A guard who is eliminated is replaced by a new one out of a stairwell after 45 seconds, after 20 seconds while the backup is on, as long as the cap allows.
    (The owner gave the replacement times for floors 1 and 2 only: floors 3 and up use the same ones for now.)
32. The hallway loudspeakers are round discs flat against the ceiling now, between the light panels (they hung on the lights).
33. When the Big Alarm goes on, red lights that spin like a police light switch on, mounted high up on the walls of the hallways and the big rooms (scattered, their backs against the wall), and the intense music plays for as long as the backup is on or the Big Alarm is.
34. Two posters hang in the Loading Docks break room (one on the wall over the desk, one on the wall the TV is on).
35. The vending machines of the Loading Docks sell five consumables as well (E at a machine): Speed Burst (run faster for 10 s, 1 minute cooldown, up to 3 in your pack), Endurance Laster (slows the stamina drain for 1 minute, 30 s cooldown, up to 3), Silent Steps (your steps are silent for 15 s, 1 minute cooldown, up to 2), Invisible (guards cannot see you for 10 s, once a round, 1 at a time) and Accuracy Cola (better accuracy with guns for 30 s, 1 minute cooldown, up to 3). Drink one with the keys 7 8 9 0 - (in that order) or with USE in the Tab window; a row above the weapon bar shows what you carry and what is running.
36. Aiming. A gun is held at your hip. Hold the right mouse button to aim, in first and in third person: the gun comes up (the sights, the red dot or the scope are looked through in first person) and a shot flies truer than from the hip (from the hip it scatters one and a half times wider). Your teammates see you aim.
37. Damage by ammunition. Light ammo (the pistol and the SMG) does 25 to an officer's body and 40 to his head; medium ammo (the rifle) 40 and 60; heavy ammo (the sniper rifle) 100; a shotgun shell does 75 to an officer in range (its eight pellets count as one hit on each officer). An officer has 100 health; a shield takes a share of every bullet. A hit to the head (the top fifth of his body) does more than a hit to the body.
38. The hit marker. A small X flashes at the crosshair when your bullet hits an officer (white, with a tick); a headshot flashes gold with a ding; the shot that puts an officer down for good flashes red, bigger, with a thump.
39. Every gun has its own hip-fire and aiming animations, standing and running (the pistol is held in both hands now): your character and your teammates' hold the pistol, the SMG, the rifle, the shotgun and the sniper rifle at the chest, or up at the sights while the right mouse button is held, and the gun moves with the run. A shot kicks the barrel up. Reloading still lowers a long gun.

If something goes wrong
 - No lobbies or friends show up: make sure Steam is running before you start the game.
 - The game keeps its log here: %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\DefaultCompany\Employees Take Revenge\Player.log
   Send that file with a description of what happened.
