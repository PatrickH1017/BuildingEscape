Floor 7 walls and ceiling - before / after pictures (2026-10-23)
================================================================

READ THIS FIRST: these are NOT screenshots from Unity. They were made OUTSIDE Unity (three.js, plain lighting)
from the real planned layout of floor 7 (seed 4101) and the real positions the new code computes for every wall
piece and ceiling tile, with the pack's own wall and ceiling models. Colours, light and shadow will look different
in the game. The pictures leave out the glass fronts of the offices, the light panels and the windows.

BEFORE = floor 7 as it is now (plain painted walls, the flat grey ceiling, the one coloured accent wall per room).
AFTER  = the same camera with the pack's wall pieces (skirting, dado rail, crown moulding) and the pack's
         suspended ceiling tiles.

                                              storage, copy room, server room, nook, conference, training, open plan)
  doorways_and_halls_before_after_1..4.png    11 views through doorways and along corridors

                                              before_<view>.png / after_<view>.png

What changed in the AFTER pictures
  - every full-height wall face is covered with the pack's 2.5 m wall pieces (stretched a little to fit)
  - the paint and the moulding follow the room: offices, meeting rooms, lounges and break rooms have a dado rail and
    skirting; corridors, storage, copy and server rooms have a plain skirting
  - each room's accent wall is still there, in the same colour, but made of the same pieces; in the lounges, nooks,
    break rooms and canteens the accent wall is the pack's BRICK wall instead of a flat colour
  - the beams over the doorways are plain pieces
  - the ceiling is a grid of 3 m tiles under the old flat ceiling
Not changed: the layout, the doorways, the colliders, the light panels, the glass fronts, the outside windows,
the guard outpost's dark walls, every other floor.
