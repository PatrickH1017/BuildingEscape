### 2.73 The owner's gun stance animations: hip fire and aimed, standing and running, for every gun (the owner, 2026-10-27) - BUILT, TESTED IN UNITY IN A SCRATCH COPY (pure + a new Play scenario with pictures, the older gun / animation scenarios, two machines) AND APPLIED; NOT PLAYED BY HAND, NOT IN A BUILD, NOT COMMITTED
**The owner's words, verbatim:** "I will now provide plenty of animations for hip-fire while standing/moving and aiming while standing/moving. Please implement them properly to the matching weapon" (the folder `Downloads\Gun_Aim_Animations`, with its own README.txt). Built on top of 2.72 (the gun is at the hip unless the right mouse button is held).

**The files.** 20 Blender 5.2 files, for the Pistol, SMG, Rifle, Shotgun and Sniper: `<Gun>_HipFire_Stand` (60 frames, a slow breathing sway), `_HipFire_Run` (20-frame cycle), `_Aimed_Stand` (60), `_Aimed_Run` (24); every file loops. Dock worker 1's skeleton, the same as the older weapon files. The gun is in the right hand exactly where the older files have it (measured with `DevTools\WeaponAnim_2026-10-27\measure.py` at frame 5 of the hip, aimed and aimed-running files: the same numbers as `WeaponAnimation.GunHolds` and `PistolAnimation.GunOrigin`, so no table changed). **The pistol is held in BOTH hands now** (the pack's README: "Say if you want one-handed versions"). The aimed poses put the gun's sight on the line of the right eye (the pack's README). **Blender:** this machine had no working Blender (the 5.2 install was gone, `Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 4.5` is empty); 5.2.1 was extracted from the owner's own `Downloads\blender-5.2.1-windows-x64.msi` (signature checked: Blender Foundation) with `msiexec /a ... /qn TARGETDIR=C:\b52` - an extraction, no install, nothing registered; a short target path is needed (the MSI's own paths are long: error 1320 otherwise). Each file was exported with `DevTools\PistolAnim_2026-10-27\export_fbx.py` (the armature and its action, the right-hand finger bones renamed for Unity) to `Assets\Resources\Animations\<Gun>_<HipFire|Aimed>_<Stand|Run>.fbx` (the game calls the SMG "Smg"). `WeaponAnimationSetup` (menu Employees Take Revenge > Setup Weapon Animations) sets the 20 files up as humanoid loops with the Synty characters' mapping and rest pose, one clip named like its file each (all 20: valid humanoid). The loop is the take without its last frame (1.967 s / 0.633 s / 0.767 s); the take's last frame is one step from its first, so nothing jumps.

**How it is in the game**
- `WeaponAnimation.GunClips.hipStand / hipRun / aimStand / aimRun` and `Stances` (the four are in the build and `WeaponAnimation.StancesEnabled`), loaded by `WeaponAnimation.LoadStances` (the long guns in `Gun(kind)`, the pistol in `PistolAnimation.Load`). The officers' pistol (`Guard()`) has no stances: it keeps the old clips.
- `CharacterAnimator`: the gun layer's mixer has 7 inputs: 0 = the raise clip at its first frame = the LOW READY (long guns: both hands on the gun, muzzle down; the pistol: at the side), 3 to 6 = the four loops. Weights: low = 1 - `AimRules.HipShare(raise)`; hip stance = HipShare x (1 - `AimShare`); aimed stance = HipShare x AimShare; each split by `runShare` (0 standing .. 1 running). `runShare` comes from the player's speed in m/s (`Locomote(..., metersPerSecond)`, `PlayerVisual` passes `v.magnitude`; shown from 1 m/s, all of it from 3.5 m/s: `StanceWalkMs`, `StanceRunMs`; smoothed at 6 a second). The standing loops run on a clock of their own; the running loops take their phase from the legs' own run (or walk) clip, so the gun bobs with the steps (`StanceRunPhase` offset 0 - tune by eye). The layer's mask is the upper body and both arms for the pistol too.
- `HeldGun` (`stances`): the barrel as the clip holds it is turned to the crosshair every frame (the chest takes half of the turn, at most 35 degrees, for a long gun and 35 % / 30 degrees for the pistol, the arms the rest; the head looks 60 % of the way along it only while aimed). **The shot's kick is made in code** (the gun's own `KickDegrees`, the barrel jumps up and comes back at 9 a second): the stance loops have no shot, and the older raise / aim / fire clips are not played for a gun that has stances. The pistol's left hand stays on the gun as the right arm turns (`AnimatedLateUpdate`). **When the left arm cannot reach the gun after the turn** (running: the body wobbles, the turn is larger and the left arm is nearly straight on a foregrip) **the gun is moved towards the left shoulder by what the arm lacks** (at most 12 cm; the first run of the test showed the left hand 2-7 cm off the gun while running). First person: the hip is the clip's own pose (the gun low at the right, its muzzle in the picture, no sight, no scope); the sights come in with the right mouse button (the red dot, the scope: unchanged); both arms are drawn for the pistol (`LocalArmMode` 2: it is held in both hands). Reload: the pistol plays its reload clip over the stance, a long gun goes down to the low ready in third person, as before.
- Not changed: the officers' guns, the melee weapons, the first-person sights (the Kinemation layer), the hip / aim rules and the numbers of 2.72.
