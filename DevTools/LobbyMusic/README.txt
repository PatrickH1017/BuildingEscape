Lobby music tooling (2026-10-17). The owner's 30 s loop (Assets\Basic Uplifting Loop) is cut up and rejoined into a 2.5 minute song: Assets\Resources\LobbyMusic.wav, played by MusicDirector in the Menu scene (Mood.Lobby). BuildLobbyMusic.ps1 re-renders it (edit the 'labels' order or the treatments to change the arrangement); AudioTools.cs is its C# helper (WAV reader/writer, FFT features, crossfade assembler, low-pass sweeps, riser, impact). The result could not be listened to by the agent that made it: joins were checked numerically (as smooth as the loop's own bar lines).
